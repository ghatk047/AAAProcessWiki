SHARED SHARED-TR-BK-05 — Car Rental Booking via GDS

This process involves booking car rentals through a Global Distribution System (GDS) for AAA Northeast members. It ensures seamless reservation and management of rental vehicles, including communication with external partners and internal systems.

L3 NameCar Rental Booking via GDS
TriggerA customer initiates a car rental request via the AAA App or directly to an AAA Northeast agent.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in a confirmed car rental booking, including vehicle details, pickup/drop-off information, and payment processing.
Org NotesCAA SCO may use different GDS partners and have additional compliance requirements due to FSRA regulation. CAA SCO also integrates with their own telematics system for enhanced tracking and reporting.
