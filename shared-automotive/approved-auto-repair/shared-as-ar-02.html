This process ensures that auto repair shops in the AAA Northeast network meet safety and quality standards through regular inspections and annual compliance audits.

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Number of shops scheduled for inspection per quarter (Target: 100 shops) Average time to assign inspectors per shop (Target: <2 hours) Number of shops inspected per day (Target: >5 shops) Time to review inspection reports per shop (Target: <4 hours) Response time for compliance notifications (Target: <24 hours)

Key Risks

Inconsistent inspector availability Difficulty accessing shop facilities Overlooked compliance issues due to lack of thoroughness Delayed communication affecting shop operations Resource constraints affecting audit coverage
