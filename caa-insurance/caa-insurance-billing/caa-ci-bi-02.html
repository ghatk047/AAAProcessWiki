CAA CAA-CI-BI-02 — Installment Plan Configuration (Monthly and Quarterly and Annual)

🍁 CAA SCO Process Reference

This CAA process mirrors AAA-IN-BI-02 in structure. The table below lists the key CAA-specific differences:

  • HST (Harmonized Sales Tax 13%) vs US sales tax
  • Ontario Insurance Act premium payment grace periods
  • FSRA regulatory reporting requirements
  • CAD currency reconciliation
  • 2% Ontario provincial tax on auto premiums
