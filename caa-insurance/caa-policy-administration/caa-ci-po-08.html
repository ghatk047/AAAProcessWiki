CAA CAA-CI-PO-08 — Guidewire Account Management Portal Policyholder Self-Service

The process enables policyholders to manage their policies through the Guidewire Account Management Portal, allowing them to view policy details, make payments, and update contact information.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDCAA-CI-PO-08
OrganisationCAA
L1 DomainCAA Insurance (Ontario)
L2 ProcessPolicy Administration (CAA)
L3 NameGuidewire Account Management Portal Policyholder Self-Service
TriggerPolicyholder logs into the CAA MyCAA App or accesses the Guidewire Account Management Portal.
OutcomePolicyholders can self-service their policy information, leading to improved customer satisfaction and reduced administrative workload for CAA staff.
Org NotesCAA South Central Ontario uses the FSRA-regulated Guidewire InsuranceSuite, ensuring compliance with Ontario regulations. The process differs from AAA in that it integrates directly with Galileo GDS for travel-related services.
SystemsGuidewire InsuranceSuite CAA MyCAA App Grasshopper Bank Salesforce
