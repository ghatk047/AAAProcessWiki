CAA CAA-CI-PO-06 — Pink Slip (OPCF 17) Issuance and Digital Delivery

The process for issuing and digitally delivering pink slips (OPCF 17) to policyholders in CAA South Central Ontario, ensuring compliance with FSRA regulations.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDCAA-CI-PO-06
OrganisationCAA
L1 DomainCAA Insurance (Ontario)
L2 ProcessPolicy Administration (CAA)
L3 NamePink Slip (OPCF 17) Issuance and Digital Delivery
TriggerA claim is approved and the vehicle is deemed totaled or a pink slip is required by law.
OutcomePolicyholders receive their pink slips digitally via the CAA MyCAA App, ensuring they have all necessary documentation for insurance claims and legal purposes.
Org NotesCAA South Central Ontario uses Guidewire InsuranceSuite for policy administration, while AAA may use different systems like Salesforce and Agero Swoop dispatch. The digital delivery method is a key difference in operational efficiency.
SystemsGuidewire ClaimCenter CAA MyCAA App Email System

1.1 Claim Approval Claims Adjuster Guidewire ClaimCenter Approved claim details Pink slip issuance request Time to approve claims < 5 days Delays in claim approval affecting pink slip issuance. N Y
1.2 Pink Slip Generation Claims Processor Guidewire ClaimCenter Approved claim details Digital pink slip (OPCF 17) Pink slip generation time < 2 hours Errors in pink slip generation leading to reissuance. N Y
2.1 Digital Delivery Preparation Claims Processor Guidewire ClaimCenter, CAA MyCAA App Digital pink slip (OPCF 17) Prepared digital delivery package Time to prepare for digital delivery < 30 minutes Technical issues with the CAA MyCAA App affecting delivery. N Y
2.2 Digital Delivery Claims Processor CAA MyCAA App, Email System Prepared digital delivery package Delivered pink slip to policyholder Delivery success rate > 95% Policyholders not receiving digital pink slips due to app issues. N Y
3.1 Confirmation of Receipt Claims Processor CAA MyCAA App, Email System Acknowledgment from policyholder Receipt confirmation record Confirmation rate > 80% Low confirmation rates due to lack of communication channels. N Y
3.2 Post-Delivery Follow-Up Claims Processor Guidewire ClaimCenter, Email System Receipt confirmation record Follow-up email or call to policyholder Follow-up response rate > 70% Low follow-up rates affecting customer satisfaction. N Y
📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Time to approve claims < 5 days Pink slip generation time < 2 hours Time to prepare for digital delivery < 30 minutes Delivery success rate > 95% Confirmation rate > 80% Follow-up response rate > 70%

Key Risks

Delays in claim approval affecting pink slip issuance. Errors in pink slip generation leading to reissuance. Technical issues with the CAA MyCAA App affecting delivery. Policyholders not receiving digital pink slips due to app issues. Low confirmation rates due to lack of communication channels.
