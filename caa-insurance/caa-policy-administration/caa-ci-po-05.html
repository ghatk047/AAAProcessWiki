CAA CAA-CI-PO-05 — Cancellation Processing (Flat and Pro-Rata and Statutory)

🍁 CAA SCO Process Reference

This CAA process mirrors AAA-IN-PO-05 in structure. The table below lists the key CAA-specific differences:

  • Guidewire PolicyCenter vs mix of PAS systems (AAA)
  • FSRA regulatory step vs state DOI filing
  • OPCF endorsement forms (Ontario-specific) vs ISO forms
  • Pink slip issuance (OPCF 17 mandatory in Ontario) vs declarations page
  • Ontario Insurance Act 15-day cancellation notice requirement
  • CAD currency vs USD
