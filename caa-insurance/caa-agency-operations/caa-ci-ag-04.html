CAA CAA-CI-AG-04 — CAA Connect Telematics Enrolment for UBI Discount

The process for enrolling customers in the CAA Connect Telematics program to qualify for UBI (Usage-Based Insurance) discounts. This involves collecting and processing telematics data, updating customer policies, and communicating with customers.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDCAA-CI-AG-04
OrganisationCAA
L1 DomainCAA Insurance (Ontario)
L2 ProcessInsurance Agency & Broker Operations
L3 NameCAA Connect Telematics Enrolment for UBI Discount
TriggerA licensed insurance agent or broker initiates this process by recommending the CAA Connect Telematics program to a customer during policy review or renewal.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in the customer being enrolled in the CAA Connect Telematics program, receiving their telematics device, and having their UBI discount applied to future insurance premiums.
Org NotesCAA South Central Ontario uses FSRA regulations and Guidewire InsuranceSuite for policy management, whereas AAA Northeast primarily uses Verisk ISO rating and Mitchell 1 for auto repair. CAA also utilizes Galileo GDS for travel services, which is not used by AAA.
SystemsGuidewire InsuranceSuite CAA Connect telematics system CAA MyCAA App Power BI FSRA System
