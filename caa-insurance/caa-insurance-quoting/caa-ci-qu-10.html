CAA CAA-CI-QU-10 — CAA Member Discount Application and Multi-Policy Bundle

The process for applying member discounts and bundling multiple policies for CAA members in Ontario. This involves validating eligibility, quoting, underwriting, and finalizing the policy bundle.

OrganisationCAA
L1 DomainCAA Insurance (Ontario)
L2 ProcessQuoting & Underwriting (CAA)
L3 NameCAA Member Discount Application and Multi-Policy Bundle
TriggerA CAA member initiates a request for a discount or multi-policy bundle through the CAA MyCAA App or by contacting a Licensed Insurance Agent.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in the issuance of a discounted policy bundle with applicable coverage, ensuring compliance with FSRA regulations and Ontario laws.
Org NotesCAA South Central Ontario focuses on member-centric services, emphasizing telematics integration for risk assessment. Unlike AAA, CAA has a stronger emphasis on local community engagement and direct agent interactions.
