SHARED SHARED-RS-DI-06 — Service Provider On-Scene Confirmation and Job Completion

This process involves confirming the arrival of a service provider at an incident scene and completing the job. It ensures that all necessary documentation is completed accurately and efficiently.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-RS-DI-06
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainRoadside Assistance
L2 ProcessDispatch Operations
L3 NameService Provider On-Scene Confirmation and Job Completion
TriggerNotification from the service provider indicating their arrival at the scene
OutcomeSuccessful confirmation of the service provider's arrival and completion of the job with all required documents finalized.
Org NotesCAA SCO uses a different telematics system (CAA Connect) compared to AAA Northeast's DriveAssist. Additionally, CAA SCO has stricter regulatory requirements due to FSRA regulations.
SystemsAgero Swoop dispatch SAP S/4HANA Guidewire ClaimCenter Salesforce (Einstein AI, Agentforce) DriveAssist telematics CAA Connect telematics

StepNameRoleSystem InputOutputKPIPain Point / Risk Decision?Exception?

1.2 Update Incident Status Dispatcher SAP S/4HANA Confirmation of arrival Updated incident status in SAP Incident status update within 1 minute Incorrect status updates leading to miscommunication N Y
2.1 Service Provider Job Completion Service Provider Agero Swoop dispatch Job completion details Completed job report in system Job completion report submitted within 30 minutes Delayed job reports affecting billing and claims processing N Y
2.2 Update Billing Information Billing Specialist SAP S/4HANA Completed job report Updated billing record in SAP Billing information updated within 1 hour Incorrect billing data leading to disputes N Y
2.3 Generate Claim Document Claims Adjuster Guidewire ClaimCenter Billing record and job details Claim document in Guidewire Claim document generated within 2 hours Missing or incomplete claim documents N Y
3.1 Finalize Incident Record Dispatcher SAP S/4HANA Claim document and billing information Finalized incident record in SAP Incident record finalized within 30 minutes of claim generation Incomplete or inaccurate final records N Y
3.2 Notify Customer Customer Service Representative Salesforce (Einstein AI, Agentforce) Finalized incident record Notification to customer Customer notification sent within 1 hour of finalization Delayed notifications affecting customer trust N Y
3.3 Review and Approve Claim Claims Adjuster Guidewire ClaimCenter Finalized incident record Approved claim in Guidewire Claim approval within 24 hours Delayed claim approvals affecting cash flow Y N
3.4 Update Telematics Data Telematics Specialist DriveAssist telematics (AAA) / CAA Connect telematics (CAA SCO) Incident details and job completion Updated telematics data in system Telematics data updated within 48 hours Inaccurate telematics data affecting future dispatch decisions N Y
3.5 Close Incident Case Dispatcher SAP S/4HANA Approved claim and updated telematics data Closed incident case in SAP Incident case closed within 2 hours of approval Open cases affecting system performance N Y
📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Response time to confirm arrival < 5 minutes Incident status update within 1 minute Job completion report submitted within 30 minutes Billing information updated within 1 hour Claim document generated within 2 hours Incident record finalized within 30 minutes of claim generation Customer notification sent within 1 hour of finalization Claim approval within 24 hours Telematics data updated within 48 hours Incident case closed within 2 hours of approval

Key Risks

Delayed service provider arrival notifications Incorrect incident status updates Delays in job completion reports Errors in billing information updates Incomplete claim document generation Inaccurate final incident record updates Late customer notifications Prolonged claim approval processes Inaccuracies in telematics data updates Open incident cases affecting system performance
