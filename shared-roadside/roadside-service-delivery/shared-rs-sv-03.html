SHARED SHARED-RS-SV-03 — Flat Tyre Service (Change or Inflate)

The Flat Tyre Service process involves identifying a vehicle with a flat tyre and providing either a spare tyre change or air inflation to ensure the vehicle can continue its journey safely.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-RS-SV-03
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainRoadside Assistance
L2 ProcessRoadside Service Delivery
L3 NameFlat Tyre Service (Change or Inflate)
TriggerA member calls the roadside assistance hotline reporting a flat tyre.
OutcomeThe vehicle is safely back on the road with a functioning tyre, and the service details are recorded in the system for billing and claims processing.
Org NotesAAA Northeast uses Salesforce for customer interaction tracking, while CAA SCO relies more heavily on their internal systems like ClientBase for such interactions.
