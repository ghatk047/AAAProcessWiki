SHARED SHARED-RS-SV-07 — EV Roadside Assistance (Mobile Charge and Tow to Charger)

This process handles the delivery of EV roadside assistance services including mobile charging and towing to a charger. It ensures that electric vehicle members receive timely and effective support when their vehicles are in need.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-RS-SV-07
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainRoadside Assistance
L2 ProcessRoadside Service Delivery
L3 NameEV Roadside Assistance (Mobile Charge and Tow to Charger)
TriggerA member contacts AAA Northeast or CAA SCO via phone, online chat, or mobile app requesting EV roadside assistance for issues like dead battery or being stranded without access to a charging station.
OutcomeThe member's electric vehicle is successfully charged and towed to a nearby charger, ensuring the member can continue their journey safely and efficiently.
Org NotesCAA SCO operates under FSRA regulations and may have additional compliance requirements compared to AAA Northeast. CAA SCO also uses different systems like ClientBase for customer management.
SystemsAAA D3 Dispatch SAP S/4HANA Agero Swoop dispatch GPS tracking

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Response time within 5 minutes Technician dispatched within 10 minutes of request Time to arrive at scene within 30 minutes Assessment completed within 10 minutes of arrival Charging completed within 45 minutes Towing completed within 30 minutes Completion confirmed within 5 minutes of towing Notification sent within 10 minutes of completion Ticket closed within 5 minutes of notification

Key Risks

Delays in response and dispatch leading to increased member dissatisfaction. Inefficient assessment causing incorrect service provision. Long charging times causing member inconvenience. Delays in towing causing member frustration. Open tickets leading to unresolved issues and potential legal liabilities.
