AAA AAA-IN-PO-02 — Policy Endorsement and Mid-Term Change Processing

This process handles the endorsement and mid-term changes for policies managed by AAA Northeast. It ensures that policy updates are accurately reflected in the system, maintaining compliance with state regulations.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDAAA-IN-PO-02
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessPolicy Administration
L3 NamePolicy Endorsement and Mid-Term Change Processing
TriggerA request for a policy endorsement or mid-term change is initiated by a Licensed Insurance Agent or Policyholder through the AAA App or Salesforce platform.
OutcomeSuccessful completion of this process results in updated policy details being recorded in Guidewire Cloud, ensuring accurate billing and claim processing.
Org NotesAAA Northeast operates under specific state regulations and uses cloud-based systems for policy management, differing from CAA's on-premises setup. The use of Salesforce for customer interaction adds an additional layer of automation and data capture.
