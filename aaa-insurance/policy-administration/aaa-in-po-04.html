AAA AAA-IN-PO-04 — Non-Renewal and Cancellation Processing

The Non-Renewal and Cancellation Processing process handles the termination of insurance policies for AAA Northeast members. It ensures that all necessary notifications, cancellations, and refunds are processed accurately and efficiently.

L2 ProcessPolicy Administration
L3 NameNon-Renewal and Cancellation Processing
TriggerPolicy expiration date reached or member initiates cancellation request through the AAA App or Salesforce portal.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in the policy being officially terminated, with accurate records updated in Guidewire Cloud, and appropriate notifications sent to the member via email or SMS.
Org NotesAAA Northeast may have specific state DOI requirements that differ from other regions. Additionally, the use of Salesforce for customer interactions adds an extra layer of automation compared to CAA's focus on Guidewire portals.
