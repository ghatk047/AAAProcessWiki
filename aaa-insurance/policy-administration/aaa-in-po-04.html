AAA AAA-IN-PO-04 — Non-Renewal and Cancellation Processing

This process handles the non-renewal and cancellation of policies for AAA Northeast members. It involves notifying customers, processing cancellations through Guidewire Cloud, and updating records in Salesforce.

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Time to initiate non-renewal/cancellation < 24 hours Case review time < 2 business days Policy status update within 1 business day Salesforce record update within 1 business day Invoice generation time < 1 business day Case closure time < 1 business day Rating update time < 1 business day

Key Risks

Delays in initiating the non-renewal/cancellation process Incorrect notifications to customers Errors in case review and approval Billing errors due to incorrect policy status updates Outdated customer records affecting future interactions Missing final invoices leading to late payments Open cases causing administrative overhead Outdated Verisk ISO ratings affecting underwriting decisions
