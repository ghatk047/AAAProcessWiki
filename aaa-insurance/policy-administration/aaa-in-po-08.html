AAA AAA-IN-PO-08 — Policy Document Delivery (Digital and Print)

This process handles the delivery of policy documents both digitally and in print for AAA Northeast members. It involves various systems to ensure timely and accurate document distribution.

Process IDAAA-IN-PO-08
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessPolicy Administration
L3 NamePolicy Document Delivery (Digital and Print)
TriggerPolicy issuance or renewal by a Licensed Insurance Agent
OutcomeDigital and printed policy documents are delivered to members, ensuring compliance with state DOI regulations.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on digital delivery as the primary method, while CAA may have more emphasis on physical document distribution due to different operational strategies.
