AAA AAA-IN-PO-01 — New Policy Issuance and Document Generation

The process for issuing new policies and generating related documents for AAA Northeast. This involves capturing policy details, underwriting, document generation, and final issuance.

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Application submission rate > 95% within 2 minutes Underwriting approval rate > 85% within 1 hour Document generation time < 5 minutes Accuracy of rating calculation > 98% Payment processing time < 10 minutes Confirmation delivery rate > 99% within 1 hour

Key Risks

Delays in underwriting review due to high volume Errors in document generation leading to rework Inaccurate premium calculations resulting in customer disputes Failed payments causing policy issuance delays Customer service issues affecting confirmation delivery rate
