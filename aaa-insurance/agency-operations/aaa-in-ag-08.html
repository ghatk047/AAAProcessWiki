AAA AAA-IN-AG-08 — AAA Approved Auto Repair Insurance Referral Process

This process handles the referral of approved auto repair services to members through AAA Northeast's insurance agency operations. It ensures that claims are processed efficiently, and members receive quality repairs.

L3 NameAAA Approved Auto Repair Insurance Referral Process
TriggerA member submits a claim for auto repair services.
OutcomeThe claim is processed, and the member is referred to an approved auto repair service with the necessary information for repair.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on direct member engagement through its licensed insurance agents, while CAA might have more centralized processing due to different organizational structures.
