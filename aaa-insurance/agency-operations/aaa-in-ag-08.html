AAA AAA-IN-AG-08 — AAA Approved Auto Repair Insurance Referral Process

The AAA Approved Auto Repair Insurance Referral Process is designed to facilitate the referral of auto repair claims to approved repair shops. This process ensures that members receive timely and quality repairs while maintaining compliance with insurance policies.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDAAA-IN-AG-08
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessInsurance Agency Operations
L3 NameAAA Approved Auto Repair Insurance Referral Process
TriggerA member submits an auto repair claim through the AAA App or directly to a licensed insurance agent.
OutcomeThe claim is processed, repaired at an approved auto repair shop, and the member receives a settlement for the repair costs.
Org NotesAAA Northeast may have specific state regulations that differ from other regions. The process also integrates with Guidewire Cloud for policy management and claims handling.

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Time to first response < 24 hours Assessment completion within 48 hours Repair shop selection within 24 hours Member notification within 1 hour of referral Verification within 7 days of repair completion Payment within 5 business days of verification

Key Risks

Delays in claim submission due to technical issues or member error Complex claim evaluations leading to extended processing times Limited availability of approved auto repair shops in certain areas Communication delays between claims adjusters and members Inaccurate repair reports from repair shops
