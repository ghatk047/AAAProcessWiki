AAA AAA-IN-BI-03 — Payment Receipt and EFT and Credit Card Processing

The Payment Receipt and EFT and Credit Card Processing process handles the receipt of payments from policyholders through various channels, including electronic funds transfer (EFT) and credit cards. It ensures accurate processing, reconciliation, and reporting of these transactions.

L2 ProcessBilling & Premium Management
L3 NamePayment Receipt and EFT and Credit Card Processing
TriggerReceipt of payment from a policyholder via EFT or credit card
OutcomeSuccessful processing of the payment, updated billing records, and generation of receipts for policyholders.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on direct interaction with policyholders through licensed insurance agents and utilizes Guidewire Cloud for policy management. The process is integrated with Salesforce for customer relationship management and Verisk for ISO rating services.
