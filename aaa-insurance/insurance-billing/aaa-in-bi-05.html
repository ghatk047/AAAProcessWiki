AAA AAA-IN-BI-05 — Agent Commission Calculation and Statement

This process calculates and generates commission statements for licensed insurance agents based on their sales performance. It ensures accurate financial reporting and compliance with state DOI regulations.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDAAA-IN-BI-05
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessBilling & Premium Management
L3 NameAgent Commission Calculation and Statement
TriggerMonthly end of the billing cycle
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces accurate agent commission statements, which are distributed to all licensed agents via email.
Org NotesThe process for AAA Northeast is tailored to meet specific state DOI requirements and uses the Guidewire Cloud platform for policy management. Unlike CAA, AAA does not have a telematics component in this process.
