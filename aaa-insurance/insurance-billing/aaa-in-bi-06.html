AAA AAA-IN-BI-06 — Refund Processing (Cancellation and Overpayment)

The Refund Processing (Cancellation and Overpayment) process handles the cancellation of policies and refunds for overpayments, ensuring accurate financial reconciliation and customer satisfaction.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDAAA-IN-BI-06
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessBilling & Premium Management
L3 NameRefund Processing (Cancellation and Overpayment)
TriggerPolicy cancellation request or detection of overpayment through billing system.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in a processed refund to the policyholder and updated records in all relevant systems.
Org NotesAAA Northeast may have specific state DOI compliance requirements that differ from other AAA regions. Additionally, the use of Guidewire Cloud for policy management is integrated with Salesforce for customer interactions.
SystemsGuidewire PolicyCenter Guidewire BillingCenter Grasshopper Bank Salesforce
📋 L4 Process Steps
StepNameRoleSystem InputOutputKPIPain Point / Risk Decision?Exception?
1.1 Policy Cancellation Request Received Licensed Insurance Agent Guidewire PolicyCenter Cancellation request form or email Updated policy status in Guidewire PolicyCenter Time to process cancellation request < 24 hours Delays in updating the policy status can lead to confusion for customers. N Y
1.2 Verify Overpayment Billing Analyst Guidewire BillingCenter Policy details and billing records Overpayment verification report Accuracy of overpayment detection > 95% Incorrect identification of overpayments can lead to financial discrepancies. Y N
2.1 Prepare Refund Billing Analyst Grasshopper Bank Overpayment verification report Refund transaction details Time to prepare refund < 48 hours Delays in refund preparation can cause dissatisfaction among customers. N Y
2.2 Process Refund Transaction Financial Analyst Grasshopper Bank Refund transaction details Processed refund receipt Success rate of refund transactions > 98% Failed transactions can lead to customer complaints and additional administrative work. N Y
2.3 Update Customer Records Customer Service Representative Salesforce Processed refund receipt Updated customer record in Salesforce Time to update customer records < 1 hour Outdated customer records can lead to incorrect information being communicated. N Y
3.1 Notify Customer of Refund Customer Service Representative Salesforce (Einstein AI, Agentforce) Updated customer record Refund notification email or SMS Customer satisfaction with refund communication > 85% Poor communication can lead to customer dissatisfaction and potential churn. N Y
3.2 Close Refund Case Billing Analyst Guidewire BillingCenter Refund notification confirmation Closed refund case in Guidewire BillingCenter Time to close refund case < 24 hours Open cases can lead to administrative overhead and potential data inconsistencies. N Y
📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Time to process cancellation request < 24 hours Accuracy of overpayment detection > 95% Success rate of refund transactions > 98% Customer satisfaction with refund communication > 85% Time to close refund case < 24 hours

Key Risks

Delays in updating policy status can lead to confusion for customers. Incorrect identification of overpayments can lead to financial discrepancies. Failed transactions can cause customer complaints and additional administrative work. Outdated customer records can lead to incorrect information being communicated. Poor communication can lead to customer dissatisfaction and potential churn.
