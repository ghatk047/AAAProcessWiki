AAA AAA-IN-BI-06 — Refund Processing (Cancellation and Overpayment)

This process handles the refund processing for cancellations and overpayments in AAA Northeast's insurance operations. It ensures timely and accurate refunds to policyholders while maintaining compliance with state regulations.

L3 NameRefund Processing (Cancellation and Overpayment)
TriggerA cancellation request is received from a policyholder or an overpayment is detected during billing.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in a processed refund being issued to the policyholder, with updated records in all relevant systems.

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Time to process cancellation request < 24 hours Accuracy of cancellation validation > 98% Accuracy of refund calculations > 99% Time to prepare refund document < 4 hours Time to issue refund < 24 hours Accuracy of record updates > 98% Response time for notification < 24 hours Time to close refund case < 48 hours

Key Risks

Delays in processing cancellation requests can lead to customer dissatisfaction. Incorrect validations can lead to financial discrepancies. Errors in refund calculations can result in over or under payments. Failed refunds can lead to customer complaints and additional processing time. Inaccurate record updates can lead to discrepancies in future billing.
