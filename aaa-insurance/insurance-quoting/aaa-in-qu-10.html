AAA AAA-IN-QU-10 — Multi-Policy Discount Calculation and Bundling

The process calculates multi-policy discounts and bundles for AAA Northeast customers using state DOI data, Guidewire Cloud, Sabre, Verisk, Salesforce, and USD.

L3 NameMulti-Policy Discount Calculation and Bundling
TriggerA customer initiates a quote request through the AAA App or Salesforce Einstein AI.
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces a bundled quote with applicable multi-policy discounts, presented to the customer for review and potential purchase.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on leveraging Guidewire Cloud for policy management while CAA SCO uses a mix of Guidewire InsuranceSuite and other systems. AAA Northeast also integrates more deeply with Salesforce Einstein AI for enhanced customer interaction.
