AAA AAA-IN-QU-01 — Auto Insurance Quote Initiation (Web and Agent and Phone)

This process initiates the auto insurance quoting and underwriting for AAA Northeast through web, agent, or phone channels. It involves capturing customer details, assessing risk, generating quotes, and finalizing the policy.

L3 NameAuto Insurance Quote Initiation (Web and Agent and Phone)
TriggerCustomer inquiry via web portal, phone call, or in-person visit by a Licensed Insurance Agent
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces a finalized auto insurance quote with terms and conditions presented to the customer for review and acceptance.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on digital integration with Guidewire Cloud for policy management, while CAA may have more emphasis on telematics and mobile apps. AAA uses Verisk ISO rating for risk assessment compared to CAA's proprietary models.
