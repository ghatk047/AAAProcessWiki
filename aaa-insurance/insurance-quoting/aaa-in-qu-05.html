AAA AAA-IN-QU-05 — ISO Rating Engine Calculation

The ISO Rating Engine Calculation process is responsible for determining the insurance premium rates based on vehicle information and risk factors. This process is initiated when a new quote request is received, and it produces a detailed quote with accurate premium calculations.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDAAA-IN-QU-05
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessQuoting & Underwriting
L3 NameISO Rating Engine Calculation
TriggerA new quote request is submitted by a customer through the AAA App or Salesforce Einstein AI.
OutcomeSuccessful completion of this process results in the generation of a detailed insurance quote with accurate premium rates, which is then presented to the customer for review and approval.
Org NotesAAA Northeast specifically uses Verisk ISO rating for premium calculations, while CAA may use different methodologies or systems. Additionally, AAA leverages Salesforce Einstein AI for enhanced customer interaction during the quoting process.

KPIs

Time to log quote request < 5 minutes Accuracy of validation > 95% Time to calculate premiums < 10 minutes Quote generation time < 5 minutes Quote approval time < 1 hour

Key Risks
