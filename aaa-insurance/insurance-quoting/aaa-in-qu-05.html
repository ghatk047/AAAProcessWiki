AAA AAA-IN-QU-05 — ISO Rating Engine Calculation

This process calculates ISO ratings for AAA Northeast policies using Verisk data and integrates the results into Guidewire Cloud for quoting and underwriting.

L3 NameISO Rating Engine Calculation
TriggerA new quote request is initiated by a Licensed Insurance Agent through Salesforce Einstein AI.
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces an accurate ISO rating that is integrated into the Guidewire PolicyCenter for further underwriting analysis.
Org NotesAAA Northeast uses Verisk ISO ratings exclusively, while CAA may use different rating engines depending on local regulations and agreements.
