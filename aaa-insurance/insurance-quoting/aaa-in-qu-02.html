AAA AAA-IN-QU-02 — Driver and Vehicle Information Capture

This process captures driver and vehicle information for quoting and underwriting purposes in AAA Northeast. It involves data collection, validation, and integration across various systems to ensure accurate risk assessment.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDAAA-IN-QU-02
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessQuoting & Underwriting
L3 NameDriver and Vehicle Information Capture
TriggerA customer initiates a quote request through the AAA App or by contacting an AAA Licensed Insurance Agent.
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces a comprehensive driver and vehicle information record, enabling accurate quoting and underwriting decisions.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on leveraging Guidewire Cloud for policy management and Salesforce for customer interaction. The process is tightly integrated with state DOI systems for compliance checks and Verisk for risk assessment.
SystemsGuidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter (Cloud) Salesforce (Einstein AI, Agentforce) Verisk ISO rating Mitchell 1 (auto repair)
