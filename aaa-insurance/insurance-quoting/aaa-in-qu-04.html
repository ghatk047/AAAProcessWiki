AAA AAA-IN-QU-04 — Credit Score Pull and Rating Tier Assignment

This process involves pulling a credit score and assigning a rating tier for AAA Northeast insurance quotes. It ensures accurate risk assessment and pricing.

L3 NameCredit Score Pull and Rating Tier Assignment
TriggerInitiated by a Licensed Insurance Agent upon customer inquiry or quote request.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in the assignment of a credit-based rating tier, which influences premium calculation and policy terms.
Org NotesAAA Northeast may have specific underwriting guidelines that differ from other AAA regions, impacting the credit score pull and rating tier assignment process.
