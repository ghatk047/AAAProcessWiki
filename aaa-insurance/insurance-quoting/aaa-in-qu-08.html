AAA AAA-IN-QU-08 — Quote Bind Conversion and Application Submission

The Quote Bind Conversion and Application Submission process involves converting a quote into a binding policy and submitting the application to the state DOI. This ensures that all necessary information is captured, validated, and processed in compliance with regulatory requirements.

L3 NameQuote Bind Conversion and Application Submission
TriggerA customer accepts a quote provided by an AAA Northeast Licensed Insurance Agent via the Salesforce Einstein AI platform.
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces a bound policy document, submitted application to the state DOI, and updated records in Guidewire PolicyCenter/BillingCenter.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on seamless integration between Salesforce for customer interaction and Guidewire Cloud for underwriting and billing processes. The use of Verisk ISO rating ensures accurate risk assessment.
