AAA AAA-IN-QU-03 — MVR Pull and Scoring

The MVR Pull and Scoring process is initiated when a customer applies for an insurance quote. The process involves pulling the Motor Vehicle Report (MVR) from state DOI systems, scoring it using Verisk, and integrating the data into the Guidewire PolicyCenter for underwriting decisions.
