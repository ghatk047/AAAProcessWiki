AAA AAA-IN-QU-03 — MVR Pull and Scoring

The MVR Pull and Scoring process involves retrieving a Motor Vehicle Report (MVR) for an applicant and scoring it to assess their driving history. This information is crucial for underwriting auto insurance policies.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDAAA-IN-QU-03
OrganisationAAA
L1 DomainAAA Insurance
L2 ProcessQuoting & Underwriting
L3 NameMVR Pull and Scoring
TriggerInitiated by a Licensed Insurance Agent when a new quote request is received from a customer.
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces a scored MVR report that informs the underwriting decision, leading to an accurate premium calculation and policy issuance.
Org NotesAAA Northeast may have specific state-specific requirements for MVR data retrieval and scoring compared to other regions. Additionally, the use of Verisk ISO rating in conjunction with Guidewire Cloud is unique to AAA operations.
SystemsSalesforce (Einstein AI, Agentforce) Guidewire PolicyCenter Sabre GDS Verisk ISO rating

KPIs

Time to receive and log quote request < 5 minutes Time to initiate MVR request < 2 minutes Time to receive MVR < 10 minutes Accuracy of MVR scoring > 95% Time to review MVR < 5 minutes Time to adjust premium < 2 minutes Time to send quote < 1 minute Quote acceptance rate > 80% Time to issue policy < 5 minutes

Key Risks

Delays in logging quote requests can lead to customer dissatisfaction. Errors in initiating the MVR request can delay underwriting. Inaccurate or delayed MVR data can impact underwriting decisions. Incorrect MVR scoring can lead to improper premium pricing. Errors in premium adjustment can lead to customer disputes. Delays in sending quotes can lead to lost business. Low quote acceptance rates can impact sales. Delays in issuing policies can lead to customer dissatisfaction.
