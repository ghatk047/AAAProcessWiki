SHARED SHARED-MB-SV-05 — Member Portal Profile and Preferences Management

This process manages the creation and maintenance of member profiles and preferences in the AAA Northeast membership management system. It ensures that members can update their information and select benefits tailored to their needs.

L3 NameMember Portal Profile and Preferences Management
TriggerMember initiates a profile update or preference change through the AAA App or online portal.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in an updated member profile with accurate personal and preference data, enabling personalized services and benefits.
Org NotesCAA SCO uses the Guidewire InsuranceSuite for policy management, while AAA Northeast primarily uses Salesforce and SAP S/4HANA. CAA SCO also integrates with ClientBase for additional client management features.
