SHARED SHARED-MB-SV-04 — Notary and Document Authentication Service

This process handles the authentication and notarization of documents submitted by AAA Northeast members. It ensures that all documents meet legal standards before they are processed further.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-MB-SV-04
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMember Services & Benefits
L3 NameNotary and Document Authentication Service
TriggerA member submits a document for authentication and notarization through the AAA App or via email.
OutcomeThe document is authenticated, notarized if required, and returned to the member with a confirmation receipt.
Org NotesCAA SCO differs in that they use their own internal notary service rather than an external provider. Additionally, CAA SCO has stricter compliance requirements for document authentication due to FSRA regulations.
