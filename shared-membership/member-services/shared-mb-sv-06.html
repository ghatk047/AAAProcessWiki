SHARED SHARED-MB-SV-06 — Lost and Stolen Membership Card Replacement

This process outlines the steps for replacing a lost or stolen membership card for members of AAA Northeast. It ensures that the member's information is securely updated and a new card is issued promptly.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-MB-SV-06
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMember Services & Benefits
L3 NameLost and Stolen Membership Card Replacement
TriggerA member reports their membership card as lost or stolen through the AAA Northeast website, mobile app, or by contacting customer service.
OutcomeThe member receives a replacement membership card with updated security features, and their account status is verified and secured.
Org NotesCAA SCO may have additional regulatory requirements due to FSRA oversight. They might also use different systems for telematics and account management portals.
SystemsSalesforce (Einstein AI, Agentforce) SAP S/4HANA Grasshopper Bank

2.2 Generate Replacement Card Membership Services Specialist Grasshopper Bank Updated member record from SAP Replacement card request sent to Grasshopper Bank Card generation request within 10 minutes Bank system delays N Y
3.1 Issue Replacement Card Membership Services Specialist Grasshopper Bank Replacement card from Grasshopper Bank Physical replacement card sent to member Card issuance within 24 hours Shipping delays N Y
3.2 Confirm Receipt of Card Member Service Representative Salesforce (Einstein AI, Agentforce) Confirmation from member Case closed in Salesforce Case closure within 30 minutes of receipt confirmation Member not responding N Y
