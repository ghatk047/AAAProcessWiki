SHARED SHARED-MB-SV-01 — Member Discount Partner Redemption

This process handles the redemption of member discounts by partners for AAA Northeast members. It involves validating partner requests, processing transactions, and updating member records.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-MB-SV-01
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMember Services & Benefits
L3 NameMember Discount Partner Redemption
TriggerA partner submits a request for discount redemption on behalf of an AAA Northeast member.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in the redemption of the discount, updated member records, and confirmation to both the partner and the member.
Org NotesCAA SCO uses a different system (Guidewire InsuranceSuite) for processing claims compared to AAA's Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter. Additionally, CAA SCO has stricter regulatory requirements due to FSRA oversight.
