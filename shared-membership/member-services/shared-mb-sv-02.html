SHARED SHARED-MB-SV-02 — Passport Photo Service Delivery (Branch)

This process handles the delivery of passport photos for AAA Northeast members. It involves initiating a request, processing the photo submission, and confirming its delivery.

L3 NamePassport Photo Service Delivery (Branch)
TriggerA member initiates a passport photo service request through the AAA App or by contacting the membership office.
OutcomeThe member receives their passport photo via email or in-person pickup at a designated branch.
Org NotesCAA SCO may have additional regulatory requirements due to FSRA oversight and different GDS systems used for processing.

Key Risks
