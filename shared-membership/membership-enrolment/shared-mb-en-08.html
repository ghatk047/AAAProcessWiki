SHARED SHARED-MB-EN-08 — Duplicate Member Detection and Merge

This process detects and merges duplicate member records to ensure accurate membership management and prevent data inconsistencies.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-MB-EN-08
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Enrolment & Sales
L3 NameDuplicate Member Detection and Merge
TriggerNew member enrollment or update request
OutcomeSuccessful detection and merging of duplicate member records, ensuring clean and accurate membership database.
Org NotesCAA SCO uses Guidewire InsuranceSuite for policy management while AAA Northeast primarily uses Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter. CAA SCO also integrates with FSRA regulations.
SystemsGuidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter SAP S/4HANA Salesforce Power BI
📋 L4 Process Steps
StepNameRoleSystem InputOutputKPIPain Point / Risk Decision?Exception?
1.1 Initiate Duplicate Detection Membership Manager Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter (AAA) or Guidewire InsuranceSuite (CAA SCO) New member enrollment data Duplicate detection report Detection rate of 95% within 10 minutes Manual review required for false positives Y N
1.2 Review Duplicate Report Membership Manager Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter (AAA) or Guidewire InsuranceSuite (CAA SCO) Duplicate detection report Approved merge list Manual review time of 5 minutes per record Time-consuming manual verification process Y N
2.1 Merge Duplicate Records Membership Manager Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter (AAA) or Guidewire InsuranceSuite (CAA SCO) Approved merge list Merged member record 90% successful merges within 2 hours Data loss or corruption during merge N Y
2.2 Update Related Systems IT Specialist SAP S/4HANA, Salesforce (AAA) or Salesforce, Power BI (CAA SCO) Merged member record Updated system records 95% successful updates within 1 hour System integration issues causing delays N Y
3.1 Verify Merged Records Membership Manager Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter (AAA) or Guidewire InsuranceSuite (CAA SCO) Merged member record Verification report 100% successful verifications within 30 minutes Manual verification errors Y N
3.2 Close Process Membership Manager Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter (AAA) or Guidewire InsuranceSuite (CAA SCO) Verification report Closed process record 95% successful closure within 10 minutes Incomplete closure leading to rework N Y
📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Detection rate of 95% within 10 minutes Manual review time of 5 minutes per record 90% successful merges within 2 hours 95% successful updates within 1 hour 100% successful verifications within 30 minutes 95% successful closure within 10 minutes

Key Risks

Data loss or corruption during merge System integration issues causing delays Manual verification errors Incomplete closure leading to rework False positives in duplicate detection
