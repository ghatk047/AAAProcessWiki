SHARED SHARED-MB-EN-06 — Payment Processing and First-Year Billing

This process handles the payment processing and first-year billing for new memberships. It ensures timely receipt of payments, accurate billing, and proper record-keeping.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-MB-EN-06
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Enrolment & Sales
L3 NamePayment Processing and First-Year Billing
TriggerNew membership application approval
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in a paid membership with an active policy and recorded billing information.
Org NotesAAA Northeast uses Salesforce for customer interactions and SAP S/4HANA for financial processing. CAA SCO primarily relies on Guidewire InsuranceSuite for both policy management and billing, with additional use of Salesforce for customer engagement.
SystemsSalesforce SAP S/4HANA
