SHARED SHARED-MB-EN-03 — Phone Enrolment via Contact Centre

The Phone Enrolment via Contact Centre process involves a contact centre agent guiding a member through the enrolment and sales process over the phone. This includes collecting necessary information, processing payments, and finalizing membership.

L3 NamePhone Enrolment via Contact Centre
TriggerA member calls the contact centre to enquire about or initiate membership enrolment.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in a new member being enrolled with active membership and payment processed.
Org NotesAAA Northeast primarily uses Salesforce for customer interactions and SAP S/4HANA for financial processing. CAA SCO also uses Salesforce but integrates more closely with Guidewire InsuranceSuite for policy management.
