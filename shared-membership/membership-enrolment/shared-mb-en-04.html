SHARED SHARED-MB-EN-04 — Affiliate and Employer Group Enrolment

This process manages the enrolment of affiliates and employer groups into AAA Northeast's membership program. It involves collecting, validating, and processing application data to ensure successful onboarding.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-MB-EN-04
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Enrolment & Sales
L3 NameAffiliate and Employer Group Enrolment
TriggerA new affiliate or employer group submits an enrolment application through the AAA App or online portal.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in the creation of a new member record in SAP S/4HANA, with access to AAA services and benefits.
Org NotesCAA SCO uses Guidewire InsuranceSuite for policy management instead of SAP S/4HANA. Additionally, CAA SCO has stricter regulatory requirements due to FSRA oversight.
SystemsSAP S/4HANA Guidewire PolicyCenter AAA App / Online Portal Salesforce
