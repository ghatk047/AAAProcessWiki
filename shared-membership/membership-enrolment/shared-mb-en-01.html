SHARED SHARED-MB-EN-01 — New Member Walk-In Enrolment (Branch)

This process outlines the steps for enrolling a new member at a branch location. It includes capturing member details, processing insurance policies, and finalizing enrollment.

Process IDSHARED-MB-EN-01
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Enrolment & Sales
L3 NameNew Member Walk-In Enrolment (Branch)
TriggerA customer visits a branch to enroll as a new member.
OutcomeThe successful completion of this process results in a new member being enrolled with active insurance coverage.
Org NotesKey differences between AAA Northeast and CAA SCO: AAA uses Salesforce for customer interactions, while CAA SCO uses ClientBase. Additionally, AAA leverages Agero Swoop dispatch for service coordination, whereas CAA SCO may use different dispatch systems.
