SHARED SHARED-MB-EN-02 — Online Membership Self-Enrolment (Web/App)

The Online Membership Self-Enrolment process allows members to enroll for AAA Northeast membership directly through the web or mobile application, streamlining the enrollment and sales process.

L3 NameOnline Membership Self-Enrolment (Web/App)
TriggerA potential member initiates the self-enrollment process by visiting the AAA Northeast website or downloading the AAA App.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in a new member account being created, with all necessary information captured and stored in the system for future reference.
Org NotesCAA SCO may require additional compliance checks due to FSRA regulations. The enrolment process might include more detailed health and safety questionnaires or telematics device installations.
