SHARED SHARED-MB-RN-08 — Membership Winback Campaign (12-36 Month Lapsed)

This process aims to initiate and execute a membership winback campaign for AAA Northeast members who have lapsed between 12 to 36 months. The goal is to re-engage these members through targeted communications and incentives, aiming to increase retention rates.

OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Renewal & Retention
L3 NameMembership Winback Campaign (12-36 Month Lapsed)
TriggerIdentification of lapsed members via the SAP S/4HANA system who have been inactive for 12-36 months.
OutcomeSuccessful completion of the winback campaign results in a significant increase in membership renewals within the target period.
Org NotesCAA SCO uses Guidewire InsuranceSuite instead of SAP S/4HANA for member management and communication. Additionally, CAA SCO has a different telematics system (CAA Connect) compared to AAA Northeast's DriveAssist.
