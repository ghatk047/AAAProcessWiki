SHARED SHARED-MB-RN-01 — Annual Renewal Notice Generation and Distribution

The process generates and distributes annual renewal notices to members, aiming to maximize membership retention by ensuring timely communication.

Process IDSHARED-MB-RN-01
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Renewal & Retention
L3 NameAnnual Renewal Notice Generation and Distribution
TriggerEnd of the membership year or a specific date set for renewal notice generation.
OutcomeMembers receive their annual renewal notices via email or postal mail, with the goal of achieving a high renewal rate.
Org NotesAAA Northeast primarily uses Salesforce and Guidewire PolicyCenter/ClaimCenter/BillingCenter for this process. CAA SCO also uses these systems but may have additional local regulations and processes due to FSRA oversight.
