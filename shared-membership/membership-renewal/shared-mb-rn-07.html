SHARED SHARED-MB-RN-07 — Renewal Rate Review and Pricing Update

The Renewal Rate Review and Pricing Update process is designed to evaluate the current renewal rates and update pricing strategies for AAA Northeast members. This ensures competitive pricing while maintaining profitability.
