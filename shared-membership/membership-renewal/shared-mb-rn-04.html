SHARED SHARED-MB-RN-04 — Membership Upgrade and Tier Change Processing

The Membership Upgrade and Tier Change Processing involves evaluating member eligibility for upgrades or tier changes based on their activity, policy details, and satisfaction scores. Successful completion results in updated membership tiers and associated benefits.

OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Renewal & Retention
L3 NameMembership Upgrade and Tier Change Processing
TriggerAutomatic trigger at the end of each renewal cycle or manual initiation by a service representative upon detecting high-value members.
OutcomeMembers receive notifications about their upgraded or changed tier status with corresponding benefits and services.
Org NotesAAA Northeast primarily uses Guidewire PolicyCenter for policy management, while CAA SCO utilizes the Guidewire InsuranceSuite. Additionally, AAA Northeast integrates Salesforce for customer interactions, whereas CAA SCO relies on ClientBase for similar purposes.

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Number of members evaluated per day - Target: 1000 Time to review and approve upgrades - Target: 1 hour per 100 members Number of tiers updated per day - Target: 500 Response time for notifications - Target: 24 hours Number of feedback received per day - Target: 50 Number of process adjustments per quarter - Target: 2

Key Risks

Data inconsistency between systems leading to incorrect evaluations. System downtime affecting the ability to update member tiers promptly. High volume of notifications causing delays in communication with members. Lack of clear communication channels resulting in misunderstandings. Resistance to change from stakeholders hindering process improvements.
