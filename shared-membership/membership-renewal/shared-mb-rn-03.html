SHARED SHARED-MB-RN-03 — Lapsed Member Reinstatement Campaign

A process to initiate and manage a campaign aimed at reinstating lapsed members by offering them special renewal packages and incentives.

📋 Process Attributes
Process IDSHARED-MB-RN-03
OrganisationSHARED
L1 DomainMembership Management
L2 ProcessMembership Renewal & Retention
L3 NameLapsed Member Reinstatement Campaign
TriggerIdentification of lapsed members through membership analytics and engagement tracking systems.
OutcomeSuccessful completion results in increased member retention, improved revenue, and enhanced brand loyalty.
Org NotesCAA SCO may have additional regulatory compliance requirements due to FSRA regulations. Additionally, CAA SCO might use different telematics data for risk assessment compared to AAA Northeast.
SystemsSAP S/4HANA Salesforce Guidewire PolicyCenter Email Marketing Platform
📋 L4 Process Steps
StepNameRoleSystem InputOutputKPIPain Point / Risk Decision?Exception?
1.1 Identify Lapsed Members Data Analyst SAP S/4HANA, Salesforce Membership database, engagement tracking data Lapsed members list Number of identified lapsed members (Target: 10% of total membership) Accurate identification based on varying definitions of 'lapse' N Y
1.2 Segment Lapsed Members Data Analyst SAP S/4HANA, Salesforce Lapsed members list Segmented lapsed members (e.g., by tenure, engagement history) Number of segments created (Target: 3-5 segments) Balancing segment size and relevance N Y
2.1 Develop Reinstatement Packages Product Manager Guidewire PolicyCenter, Salesforce Segmented lapsed members data Customized reinstatement packages Number of unique package designs (Target: 2-3 packages per segment) Balancing cost and attractiveness N Y
2.2 Create Campaign Materials Marketing Manager Salesforce, Adobe Creative Suite Reinstatement packages Campaign emails, landing pages, social media posts Number of campaign materials created (Target: 1-2 per package) Consistent branding and messaging N Y
2.3 Launch Campaign Marketing Manager Salesforce, Email Marketing Platform (e.g., MarketMuse) Campaign materials Launched campaign with tracking URLs Campaign launch date and time (Target: within 1 week of identification) Timing and reach N Y
3.1 Monitor Campaign Performance Data Analyst Salesforce, Google Analytics Campaign tracking URLs Performance metrics report Open rate, click-through rate (Target: Open > 20%, Click-Through > 15%) Low engagement rates Y N
3.2 Adjust Campaign Strategy Marketing Manager Salesforce, Adobe Creative Suite Performance metrics report Adjusted campaign materials or launch strategy Number of adjustments made (Target: 1-2 per campaign) Ineffective messaging Y N
3.3 Follow-Up with Non-Respondents Customer Service Representative Salesforce, Agero Swoop dispatch Non-responder list Engagement logs and follow-up records Number of follow-ups conducted (Target: 50% of non-respondents) High no-response rates Y N
3.4 Evaluate Campaign Success Data Analyst SAP S/4HANA, Salesforce Campaign performance data and follow-up records Campaign success report Reinstatement rate (Target: 5% of lapsed members) Low reinstatement rates Y N
3.5 Close Campaign and Report Findings Project Manager Salesforce, SharePoint Campaign success report Final campaign report and lessons learned document Report completion date (Target: within 1 week of campaign end) Detailed reporting N Y
📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Number of identified lapsed members (Target: 10% of total membership) Open rate, click-through rate (Target: Open > 20%, Click-Through > 15%) Reinstatement rate (Target: 5% of lapsed members)

Key Risks

Inaccurate identification of lapsed members Low engagement rates from campaign materials High no-response rates from follow-ups
