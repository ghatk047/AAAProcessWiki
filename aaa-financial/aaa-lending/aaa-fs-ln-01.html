AAA AAA-FS-LN-01 — Auto Loan Application and Pre-Approval

The Auto Loan Application and Pre-Approval process involves collecting loan application data, verifying eligibility, and providing pre-approval to potential borrowers. This process ensures that AAA Northeast can offer competitive financial products while maintaining compliance with state regulations.

L3 NameAuto Loan Application and Pre-Approval
TriggerA customer submits an auto loan application through the AAA App or a licensed insurance agent.
OutcomeSuccessful completion produces a pre-approved loan amount and terms, communicated back to the borrower via the AAA App or by email.
Org NotesAAA Northeast focuses on direct customer engagement through its app and mobile presence, while CAA may rely more on in-person interactions and traditional channels.

📊 KPIs & Risk Register

KPIs

Application submission rate (per hour) - Target: 10 applications/hour Data validation accuracy (95%) - Target: 95% Average credit check response time (5 minutes) - Target: 5 minutes Eligibility approval rate (80%) - Target: 80% Customer notification response time (24 hours) - Target: 24 hours Document upload completion rate (90%) - Target: 90% Final approval rate (75%) - Target: 75% Agreement signing rate (85%) - Target: 85% Funding disbursement time (5 business days) - Target: 5 business days Follow-up response rate (90%) - Target: 90%

Key Risks

High volume of submissions during peak times leading to delays Inconsistent data entry causing errors in validation Delays in receiving credit reports affecting eligibility decisions Complex eligibility criteria slowing down the approval process Technical issues with email notifications preventing timely communication Difficulty in uploading large files by borrowers Manual review process causing delays in final approval Confusion in understanding loan terms leading to low agreement signing rates Delays in bank processing affecting funding disbursement times Low engagement from borrowers resulting in missed follow-up opportunities
